Common questions

How do exchange rates update?

The app retrieves current exchange-rate information when it can connect to the internet. Rates may change and may differ from the rate offered by a bank or payment provider.

Can I use the app offline?

The app may display the last retrieved exchange rate when offline. Check the rate's update time before relying on it.

How do I report a problem?

Email spalatty@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Please do not include sensitive financial information.
