Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

DollarToRupeeConvert helps users convert between US dollars and Indian rupees. This policy describes how information may be handled when you use the app.

Information you enter

The currency amounts you enter are used to calculate conversions. The app is not intended to require an account or collect your name, address, or payment details.

Exchange-rate requests

The app retrieves exchange-rate data from an external service over the internet. When your device contacts that service, the service may receive technical information such as your IP address, request time, and device/network information according to its own practices. The provider may process request information under its own privacy policy.

Local storage

The app may retain the latest exchange rate on your device so that a previously retrieved rate can be displayed offline. Local storage is not the same as sending personal information to the app developer.

Analytics, advertising, and sharing

The app privacy disclosures should reflect any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or other third-party services included in the app. This policy will be updated if those practices change.

Children

The app is a general-purpose currency conversion utility and is not designed to solicit personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if app features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, email spalatty@gmail.com or visit our Support page.
